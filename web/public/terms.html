1. Service

MOTIS explorer gives access to public transport information (stops, departures, journeys, lines, shared mobility) through an MCP server (https://errance.litote.org/mcp), used by AI assistants, and a web chat (https://errance.litote.org/). It is free, non-commercial, and requires no account. Using the service means accepting these terms.

3. Information provided

The data comes from Transitous, which aggregates open data published by transport operators and authorities (GTFS, GTFS-RT, GBFS), and from other open data sources. It may be incomplete, outdated or wrong, and real-time information is not available everywhere. The answers of the web chat and of the assistants are generated by AI models and may contain errors. Always check the information with the transport operator before travelling, especially for accessibility, disruptions and tickets. The service sells no tickets.

For an intensive or commercial use, run your own instance: the software is open source (see section 7) and the Transitous usage policy applies.
